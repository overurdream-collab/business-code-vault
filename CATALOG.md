## Categories

- **Identity & Access** — login, password reset, sessions, users, roles, permissions.
- **Organization & Employees** — companies, branches, departments, employee records.
- **Workflow & Approvals** — document flows, approval steps, delegation, decision history.
- **Documents & Files** — document handling, uploads, attachments, exports.
- **Notifications** — email, in-app alerts, messaging integrations.
- **Audit & Logs** — activity history, change tracking, audit utilities.
- **Reports & Utilities** — reusable reports, dashboards, validators, formatting, common utilities.
- **Integrations** — reusable external API connectors and adapters.

## Status definitions

- **Candidate** — identified in a source project; not yet accepted into the vault.
- **Imported** — copied into the vault; review or testing is incomplete.
- **Verified** — documented and tested in its source context.
- **Reuse-tested** — copied into a different project and successfully tested there.
- **Deprecated** — retained for history but not recommended for new use.

A module must not be marked **Verified** or **Reuse-tested** without recorded test evidence.
